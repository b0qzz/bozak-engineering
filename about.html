About Bozak Engineering

A Kenyan supplier of ICT network equipment, working between institutional buyers and manufacturers.

Our story

Bozak Engineering Limited was founded in 2024 to help organizations in Kenya buy ICT equipment with less risk. Large buyers such as government agencies, corporates and hospitals often need goods that only manufacturers make. We act as the bridge, sourcing the product, confirming the specification and delivering it.

We focus on tender and project supply, where clear documentation and dependable delivery matter most.
