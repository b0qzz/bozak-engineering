What procurement teams usually ask

Short answers, and what we can provide on request.

Do you buy direct from manufacturers or through distributors?

Confirm sourcing route for each product on your quotation. Verified manufacturer or distributor authorizations will be published on the Resources page.

How do you handle delivery and logistics?

We coordinate shipment and delivery to your site in Kenya. Lead times are stated on each quotation.

What are your payment terms and warranty?

Payment milestones and warranty periods are set per order and written into the quotation.

Can you provide compliance documents?

Ask us for the documents your tender requires. Company documents are listed under Resources as they are verified.
